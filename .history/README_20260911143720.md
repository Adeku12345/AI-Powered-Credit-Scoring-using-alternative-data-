## AI-POWERED CREDIT SCORING USING ALTERNATIVE DATA

Softint AI is an end-to-end production platform that fairly scores creditworthiness for everyone — including those with no prior credit history. Instead of relying on a single bureau or black-box algorithm, 8 specialised AI agents independently evaluate an applicant's behaviour — producing a mathematically weighted, transparent, and legally defensible decision in seconds.
✅ Open Banking Native · ✅ FCA CONC/MCOB Compliant · ✅ GDPR Explainable · ✅ Equality Act Audited · ✅ UK + Africa Ready
🔴 The Problem
Traditional credit scoring is incomplete, opaque, expensive, and exclusionary:
5.4 million UK adults are "thin-file" — unscorable by traditional bureaus; billions globally have no credit footprint
Black-box decisions: applicants get "declined" with no explanation — violating GDPR Right to Explanation
Single-source risk: scores rely on 1–2 bureaus; errors ruin lives; updates lag by weeks
Compliance costs: FCA rules, affordability checks, and fairness audits require expensive manual review
Global gap: credit penetration below 10% across Africa — bureaus simply don't exist
✅ The Solution
Don't just look at debt history. Look at behaviour. Explain every decision. Include everyone. Comply automatically.
